# Magnitude growth pruning intervention v1

Prospective intervention motivated by the retrospective analysis; the overall project was not preregistered. The five compatible original unpruned32E source trajectories are included without performance selection. Branch at1000; past-only history250,500,1000. No future weights or IMP masks enter selection.

Current-small is the within-tensor bottom half. Fifty narrow rank strata cover that half. Sustained observed magnitude growth means both observed intervals increase; the top quartile of positive RMS-normalized OLS slopes defines growing candidates. Stable candidates are the bottom quartile of RMS-normalized total trajectory variation, with overlapping growth candidates excluded. Stable-by-net-change fluctuations do not qualify by definition. Exact quantiles, tie handling and matching are in registration.json. No individual-weight denominator is used.
