# Online reinforcement pruning v2 — frozen protocol

## Registration and prior exposure

This is a new study, distinct from v1, reusing its six dense step-250 prefixes and scores only after verification. V1 high selections are candidate solutions, not presumed valid. Before this registration, the investigator had seen v1's failed seed7/A untouched runner log, including validation losses through local step 250. That run reset the optimizer and router and was not a valid continuation. No valid v1 pruned branch outcome is known. This exposure must be disclosed in all reports. No v2 branch losses were generated or examined before this freeze.

## Cohort and intervention

Use seeds 7, 17, 29 and orders A, B; 256 expert fc1/fc2 matrices in the existing 32-expert model. Prefixes must pass provenance, schema and restored step-251 oracle checks. For attempts 1–250, R = initial-sign times sum of actual AdamW task-driven displacement divided by sum of its absolute values, excluding decoupled decay. A skipped AMP attempt contributes zero. Exclude zero initial signs and zero denominator. Verify the archived score convention and attempt/applied counts. Router and shared weights remain trainable.

For each matrix of N=262144 weights, rank by (absolute step-250 magnitude, flat index), take the first floor(N/4), then remove ineligible entries. Divide this eligible sequence into exactly five bins using numpy.array_split: the first eligible_count % 5 bins have one extra entry. Delete 13107 distinct indices in each matrix, 3355392 per cell per pruned arm. Reproduce the archived random arm using numpy.random.default_rng with seed equal to the big-endian integer represented by the first eight bytes of SHA-256 over UTF-8 `moe_online_reinforcement_pruning_v1|random|{seed}|{order}|{tensor}`, with numpy choice(pool, 13107, replace=False), then sort the indices. Never redraw.

## High selection and gates

For each bin independently maximize the sum of the stored float64 reinforcement values over binary candidate edges, subject to row-bin and column-bin degrees equal to the random draw. Each edge has capacity one. The bipartite incidence matrix is totally unimodular; continuous bounded linear programming is equivalent to integer flow when the returned solution is integral. Use IEEE binary64 scores and objective; do not quantize. Use SciPy 1.11.4 HiGHS dual simplex, candidate columns sorted by flat index, fixed row then column constraint ordering, one solver thread, presolve enabled. These fixed inputs and solver algorithm define deterministic tie handling; on numerical nonintegrality above 1e-7 or solver failure, stop rather than selecting a different optimum. An independently solved LP objective certificate must be within 1e-5 absolute of the saved selection objective for each matrix and its primal constraints must be exact. V1 candidate indices may be reused only if they equal this deterministic solution and pass all independent checks; otherwise use the registered solver's solution. Preserve solver versions, options, objective values and independent primal/dual certificates.

Acceptance checks, not additional optimization constraints: each matrix's absolute removed squared-weight-energy difference must be no more than 5% of random removed energy, requiring exact equality when random energy is zero; each cell's high-minus-random mean R >= 0.20 and overlap fraction <= 0.30. If an optimum fails, report failure; do not adjust bins, costs, objective, ties or random draw. Independently audit saved indices, score eligibility, counts, row/bin and column/bin totals, energy, overlap, score separation, and optimality. Report per-neuron energy discrepancies. Check the solver against exhaustive enumeration on a small example. Seal all six accepted selection pairs before any pruned branch.

## Continuation and measurements

All arms restore the complete step-250 model including router and buffers, AdamW groups, state and counters, scaler, scheduler or explicit absence, all RNG states, and next batch position. Verify live parameter attachment and reproduce the archived untouched step-251 oracle through the production entry point. All branches start with identical batch order and random state. Pruned arms zero only selected weights and their corresponding optimizer moments at intervention, mask gradients before global clipping, and enforce zero weights and moments after each update and restart. Untouched does not prune. Verify deletion effects and uninterrupted versus resumed short pruned runs through the production entry point. Use fresh GPU admission: at least 24 GiB free, utilization at most 90%, one admitted worker per device, without killing other processes.

Evaluate the same validation tokens before masking and immediately after masking at global 250 with no training update, then at global 251, 275, 500, 1000 and 2500. Preserve training RNG, batch position and model mode across evaluation. Local continuation attempt 0 is global 250, local 1 is global 251, local 2250 is global 2500. Record attempted and applied optimizer updates separately, including AMP skips. Checkpoint atomically with full state, arm identity, mask hash and batch position. Completion requires verified final checkpoint and validation artifacts, not merely a marker file.

## Analysis

Per cell, primary contrast is validation CE at 2500 for high minus random. Report all six, A/B mean within each seed and the unweighted mean of the three seed means. Seeds are independent replicates. Also report each pruned arm's excess over untouched, paired trajectories including immediate and final damage, directional consistency, effect sizes and heterogeneity; plot paired differences. A positive lasting contrast supports protection of groups of small weights at this checkpoint and dose. An immediate penalty that disappears supports short-term sensitivity. Do not infer Movement Pruning superiority or routing causality without separate comparisons.
