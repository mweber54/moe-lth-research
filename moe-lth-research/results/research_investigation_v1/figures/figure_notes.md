# Figure provenance and interpretation

All figures have PNG (220 dpi), PDF and SVG versions. Data are observed checkpoint/diagnostic artifacts; only the explicitly gray Gaussian spectral-null band is simulated. No missing experimental values are interpolated or imputed.

1. **01_gate_intervention_all_contrasts:** signed target-minus-control sparse-minus-dense CE contrasts from `gate_contrasts_reproduced.csv`, independently calculated from condition losses. Each color is one of seeds 7/17/29. The same common donor readout is used for both training policies. The change combines forward coefficients and backward task derivatives; it does not isolate either one. All four original contrasts are shown, including unfavorable baselines. Mean attenuation is the equal-seed mean of magnitude ratios with denominator epsilon 1e-12, not an estimate from twelve independent experiments.
2. **02_embedding_hypothesis_challenge:** independent checkpoint calculations from `embedding_geometry.csv`, `embedding_paired_changes.csv`, and `unique_embedding_spectra.csv`. Five trajectories per sparsity are retained; exact seed values are in the CSVs. The gray central 95% range is the finite Gaussian *null distribution* over 64 matrices, not uncertainty over experimental seeds. Dots in the neighbor endpoint panel are five seeds. The 2500 marker is an optimizer/scaler reset with unchanged weights. Line segments connect observations; no unobserved onset is inferred.
3. **03_fixed_probe_coupling:** directly recomputed from the 128 saved fixed-source all-expert positions per layer. Five colored seed contrasts at each sparsity use the same 128 layer/expert units in all four endpoint cells. Unmatched-expert controls use the same inputs and all other expert pairings. Equal-expert and equal-seed weighting is explicit; there are no token/expert confidence intervals.
4. **04_alternatives_and_falsification:** audited-table reaggregation, not a new full expert-weight SVD. Every source-overlap dot is a seed/age/sparsity condition, with dependent jittered ages and two sparsities; independent n remains five. The winner-hit bars summarize six architecture/seed groups drawn from three seed IDs and are not six independent trials. Neither nearest-neighbor changes nor subspace motion is causal evidence of usefulness.

5. **05_gate_training_evolution:** contrasts recomputed from common_readout_snapshot_losses.csv, intersected across all three training levels. Observed steps are marked. Solid lines denote assignment replay; dashed lines denote added detached coefficients; color identifies seed. The native level defines coverage but is omitted from the plotted comparison to retain a legible incremental intervention. Curves are observational summaries of the recorded interventions, not precise causal onset estimates.
