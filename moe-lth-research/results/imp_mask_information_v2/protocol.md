## Degree-preserving sampler and admission diagnostics

Use binary bipartite double-edge swaps within each individual expert matrix. A proposal selects two existing edges, rejects same row/column or occupied crossed edges, otherwise replaces them by the opposite corners. Maintain the existing-edge list consistently. Record proposals, accepted swaps, rejection rates and survivor overlap with IMP for every matrix. Fixed proposal budgets retain rejected self transitions; never stop chains after a fixed number of accepted swaps. Independent PRNG streams are separately initialized from the original IMP mask; this does not establish independent samples from a stationary distribution.

Before production, test two diagnostic chains per source seed/matrix at cumulative proposal lengths 2,5,10,20 times the matrix's initial surviving count. For each matrix and both chains require: exact degrees; accepted swaps at 20x at least twice the surviving count; final overlap with original survivors at most 0.5; absolute change of original-survivor overlap between 10x and 20x at most 0.02; absolute centered binary correlation between the 10x and 20x masks at most 0.20; and absolute between-chain final survivor-overlap difference at most 0.02. These prespecified thresholds are empirical admission diagnostics, not a mixing-time theorem. Production chains use 20x proposals from original IMP with five distinct deterministic seeds; require identical degree and within-chain diagnostics (10x to 20x) and distinct content hashes. Stop Phase 2 without training its masks if any diagnostic or production matrix fails, or a nontrivial matrix has no accepted randomization. Degenerate constraints are recorded and cause a phase stop rather than training near-copies. Diagnostics can be conservative when degree heterogeneity forces overlap; report that limitation rather than relax thresholds after outcomes.

No claim of uniform sampling, proven stationarity, or independent degree-mask draws is made. The sampler's symmetric proposals support a conventional swap chain, but finite chains and graph-specific mixing remain unresolved. Save compiler/runtime identity and implementation hash. No degree-mask recovery launches until all diagnostic and production masks pass.
