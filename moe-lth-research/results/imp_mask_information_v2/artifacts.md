# Artifact locations and completion semantics

- `protocol.md`, `registration.json`, `manifest.json`, `seal.json`: frozen scope, pre-outcome execution rule, all 55 exact job identities (30 replication endpoints plus 25 conditional degree endpoints), implementation identities.
- `launch.json`: actual detached host/PID records. `launch_failed_01.json` and `launch_correction.json` preserve the corrected pre-worker startup error; no recovery started in that failed launch.
- `workers/<host>.heartbeat.json`: PID, active job and timestamp, updated every 15 seconds. `status/<job>.json` is the authoritative atomic job state; `completion.json` and `job_status.csv` summarize it.
- `results.csv`: every verified endpoint, its seed/draw, absolute validation CE, historical same-budget dense CE, attempted/applied updates, AMP skips and curve/checkpoint references. Reused observations are explicitly marked. Pending runs do not appear as observations.
- `paired_effects.csv`, `aggregate_effects.json`: within-seed mask effects and equal-weight source-seed descriptive uncertainty. Independent model sample size is five source seeds, not twenty-five mask draws.
- `recoveries/<job>/attempt_0001/metrics.jsonl`: full validation recovery curve including initialization. `gradient_stats/gradient_stats.jsonl` within each attempt contains the training curve (`train_cross_entropy`), auxiliary-inclusive `train_loss`, gradient/update diagnostics and AMP flags for every attempted update. These raw training curves are retained in full. `routing_stats/` holds utilization and routing diagnostics. Compatible seed17 files remain at their original v1 paths recorded in results/manifest.
- `recoveries/<job>/attempt_0001/checks.json`, `result.json`, `stage.json`, `checkpoints/`, `component_checkpoints/`: per-condition checks, outcomes, provenance, final model and initialization component identities. No failed attempt is overwritten or retried.
- `configs/<job>.json`, `logs/<job>.log`: exact recovery input/configuration and per-job console log. `masks/`, `mask_validation.csv`, `reuse_checks.json`, `initialization_identity_checks.json`, `host_checks/`: saved masks and count/source/initialization/data verification.
- `recovery_curves.csv`, `figures/`: completed-run validation curves and endpoint/paired-effect comparisons. Raw per-attempt training curves are available as above.
- `replication_gate_evaluation.json`, `phase2_gate.json`: appear when replication finishes; exactly the preregistered all-five-seeds rule. If admitted, `degree_sampler_runtime.json`, `degree_diagnostics.json`, `degree_masks/` and `degree_preparation_status.json` record fixed proposal lengths, accepted/rejected swaps, overlap, decorrelation, exact degrees, persistence checks and finite-chain limitations. No degree recovery begins until all diagnostic and production masks pass.
- `findings.md`: live report, finalized automatically only after the bounded phases complete or the protocol's expansion rule stops further work. Check `completion.json.scientifically_complete` and failure status before treating it as final.

No existing v1 result or historical source artifact is modified. No new architecture, sparsity, discovery trajectory, router swap or adaptation factorial is dispatched.
