# Figure definitions and populations

- `coupling_sparse_dense.png`: solid sparse/dashed dense, source-seed colors, rerun trajectories. Each contrast fixes its valid layer–expert membership across all measured times. Native/current-accepted panels retain only 11–14 of 128 units, so their estimand differs from the broader four-endpoint contrast in the primary table. This coverage sensitivity can reverse effect signs. The title, findings and `all_time_population_sensitivity.csv` state the restriction explicitly. Counts and exclusions appear in `coupling_trajectories.csv`; Pearson and Spearman/count-weighted endpoint contrasts are separate CSV fields.
- `weight_geometry.png`: full-spectrum normalized Gram distance (solid same-step dense; dashed fixed dense final), then SVD overlap at ranks 8, 16 and 32 separately. SVD trajectories fix valid units across the whole track and preserve numerical-rank/cutoff exclusions. Gram metrics use all valid full-spectrum matrices and do not depend on the displayed SVD rank. Endpoint tables use reference-specific endpoint-common populations, which can differ from trajectory populations.
- `embedding_and_depth.png`: E is the tied byte embedding/LM head. Solid sparse/dashed dense drift is relative to E(0); the vertical line marks the optimizer reset. Bottom panels compare sparse and same-step dense representations at the final endpoint: E = token embedding, E+P = embedding plus position, A_l = residual after attention, N_l = normalized router input, B_l = residual after block. Each dot is a stage, not an independent replication. Token-frequency-weighted probe representations are distinct from the unweighted 256-row embedding-matrix analysis.
- `coupling_function_vs_loss.png`: individual source seed/sparsity conditions only. Left: native fixed-initial association difference-in-changes on identical fixed token–expert pairs. Right: mean complete expert relative output error on identical fixed-source hidden vectors. CE gaps use the rerun's own logged TF32-on validation evaluation. The two sparsities within a seed are dependent; the figure is descriptive and fits no regression.

Historical common states are tensor-identical to reruns and are not drawn as additional independent trajectories. All ten sparse conditions and the five unique dense controls remain in the tables. Dense curves are repeated only to show the matched comparison in each sparsity panel.
