# E32/IMP80 paired magnitude versus matched-sign recovery preregistration

Dated 2026-11-12 (America/Indianapolis). This is a new, prospectively registered experiment authorized by the user's matched-distance study request. It does not amend or extend Stage 1; preserve commits 1f60d90 and 3dae414 unchanged. Stage 1 completed 54 runs and 135,000 attempted updates before this registration. No runs of this new experiment may precede this commit.

Question: at the same per-matrix weight displacement, does perturbing survivor sign placement produce greater IMP-specific reopening during recovery than perturbing survivor magnitudes? Cohort is E32/IMP80 only: 32 experts, nominal final sparsity .8 (actual .7999992370605469), true original step-zero initialization including the original dense router, shared parameters and biases. No E16 comparison. Discovery mask came from iterative pruning; this is not a one-shot dense-to-80% mask.

## Design and fixed perturbations

Seeds 7,17,29; masks imp,random1,random2; draws 1,2,3; data orders A,B; arms M,Bm. Exactly 108 new recoveries; at most 2,500 attempted updates each, 270,000 total including AMP-skipped updates. Use the verified original recoveries as mask/seed/order-specific controls. Reuse the original first-segment optimizer reset, learning rate, precision, data orders, validation tokens and recovery code. Only initialization is perturbed; survivor weights, router and shared parameters train normally. Evaluate FP32 CE at steps 0,250,500,1000,1500,2000,2500. There is no second segment, retry, extension, early efficacy stop, added seed or other arm.

For each seed/mask/draw/expert fc1 or fc2 matrix, M independently permutes values within strictly positive survivors and within strictly negative survivors, using the audited full-dose A algorithm (two random permutations per sign group). This preserves every sign and the magnitude multiset within each sign group. Exact-zero survivors stay fixed. Masked coordinates stay zero; masks and row/column survivor counts are unchanged. Biases and all nonmasked parameters stay exactly original.

Bm uses the audited step-zero matched_signs.py algorithm: independently randomize positive and negative survivor lists, pair them in that order up to the smaller group size, compute cumulative costs 4*(w_positive^2+w_negative^2), including zero cost for no pairs, and select the prefix with squared displacement nearest the paired M squared displacement. Flip both signs in each selected pair. Do not search over alternative lists, optimize loss, tune signs, or correct a mismatch by changing magnitudes. This preserves every magnitude, sign counts, zero survivors, and matrix squared norm. M and Bm are paired by seed/mask/draw/matrix, with Bm targeting that exact M draw's realized d=||W'-W||_F/||W||_F, computed in float64. No common .35 target is imposed. RNG seed is SHA256 of matched-sign-magnitude-recovery-v1|E32|arm|seed|mask|draw|matrix, first eight bytes little endian modulo 2^63, CUDA generator; log seeds and permutation/selected-index hashes. A/B orders reuse byte-identical perturbed weights. Draw indices form registered pairs across arms and masks; random masks remain separate fixed masks, not new replicates.

Hard distance tolerance fixed before preparation: abs(d_Bm-d_M)<=0.001 in EVERY matrix, and <=0.001 for the aggregate survivor-weight distance. Record relative errors as descriptive quantities too. Stop the whole experiment before training if any match or invariant fails; do not relax tolerance, regenerate draws, or train a passing subset. Log matrix and model distances, energies, zero counts and sign-flip fractions before dispatch. Assert sign/magnitude/mask invariants and unchanged shared state. Reproduce all nine original step-zero models on fixed validation (and both order references); stop if CE differs by >1e-3. Identity must reproduce exactly.

## Estimands and paired intervals

For seed s, arm a, mask m, draw j and order o, P_a,m,j,o(t)=CE_a,m,j,o(t)-CE_original,m,o(t). I_a,j,o(t)=P_a,imp,j,o(t)-[P_a,random1,j,o(t)+P_a,random2,j,o(t)]/2. Keep the two random masks distinct and equally weighted. First calculate within each same recovery run the loss change 2000 minus 250 and subtract the corresponding original-control change. Apply the mask contrast to obtain R_a,j,o=I_a,j,o(2000)-I_a,j,o(250). Average A/B within draw, never count orders as independent draws. L_a,j is the paired-order mean I_a,j,o(2000).

Primary paired draw contrast C_s,j=R_Bm,s,j-R_M,s,j. Primary per-seed estimate C_s=mean over the three paired draw contrasts. Preserve all endpoint, arm, order and mask pairing throughout. No subtraction of independently calculated confidence intervals. Report each of the three individual draw values, arm R and L, both interaction endpoints, absolute IMP/random1/random2 losses, and each mask penalty, by seed/order as well as paired-order summaries.

For every per-seed C, arm R and arm L, use the two-sided 95% Student t interval mean +/- 4.30265272974946 * sample_SD/sqrt(3), df=2, computed from its three complete paired draws. These intervals are conditional on that source seed, the three tested masks and two fixed orders; they do not estimate population uncertainty over seeds or masks. Do not pool seeds as independent draws or infer a difference from one arm passing and the other failing. There is no multiplicity-adjusted simultaneous coverage claim. Report point estimate and interval even when imprecise.

Practical direct-comparison margin delta=0.02486047 nats, a prospective design choice, not an empirical noise bound for C or R. A seed supports a practically larger sign effect iff C_s>delta AND lower95(C_s)>0. The point estimate checks practical size; the lower bound checks direction and need not exceed delta. Seed-consistency rule: at least two of three seeds support the direct comparison AND all three C_s>0. Report each seed regardless of consistency. Mixed signs or imprecision do not establish no difference. There is no authorization to add seeds to resolve uncertainty.

Descriptive arm reopening: R_a>delta and lower95(R_a)>0. 'M near zero' requires the full R_M interval to lie inside [-delta,+delta], an equivalence description, rather than a nonsignificant test. Report both-reopen, Bm-only, M-only, neither, near-zero and unresolved outcomes without replacing the primary direct contrast. If Bm reopens, M is near zero and the direct consistency rule passes, the permitted interpretation is that sign placement matters beyond displacement under these interventions. If both reopen and the direct rule passes, both arrangements matter, with greater measured sign effect. If contrasts are imprecise/similar or neither reopens, do not claim sign-specificity at this dose. A full sign-shuffle comparison is contextual only and is not a matched-dose causal contrast.
