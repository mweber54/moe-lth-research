# Development feasibility study: incomplete

The exact-source corpus and historical batches pass the CPU provenance audit. This does **not** establish whether group-gradient consistency has adequate coverage or changes constrained pruning selections: no gradient pilot or gradient replay has been performed. The shared GPU reported 100% utilization at inspection, above the original study's 90% admission limit. No training, optimizer updates, pruning interventions, or continuation runs were performed.

The reconstructed training corpus has SHA-256 `7aad299a6af6f1c85e6ee399ed9d83156ea54af98f1cd5d69a2bf614bde1aed3`, matching the existing corpus exactly. Byte labels use source chunks and mark interleaving separators unknown. For all six seed/order combinations, the first 500 native batches match the reconstructed indices and tokens exactly, with all available archived prefix hashes verified. Full-state checkpoints and score files passed their archived hashes for all 12 cells; five step-250 checkpoints are read from the predecessor archive, as in the native study.

Across 384 matrix/checkpoint cells, the mean per-matrix Spearman correlations on the eligible population are -0.0006 for magnitude versus absolute movement, 0.2498 for magnitude versus reinforcement, and 0.0630 for movement versus reinforcement. These are descriptive averages, not seed-level uncertainty estimates. Top-k columns in `baseline_disagreement.csv` compare the largest values of each measure at k=13,107; they do not constitute pruning masks. At step 250, the archived constrained random and high-reinforcement masks differ on average by 71.92% of the maximum symmetric difference (192 matrices); this comparison is not a group-gradient result.

`group_coverage.csv` counts target positions and causal-context crossings only. For example, seed 7/order A through step 250 has 2,027,652 TinyStories targets, 2,067,812 WikiText targets, and 536 unknown separator targets. Gradient coverage, gate-weighted activation, matched random uncertainty, constrained comparisons for other scores, and cross-seed/order gradient consistency remain unmeasured. `gradient_consistency.pt` and `mask_disagreement.csv` have deliberately not been created because no valid measurements exist.

Before looking at gradient results, the proposed aggregation is the minimum across domains of the absolute mean of resolved batch-level mean-gradient signs; a weight must meet specified per-domain occurrence and contributing-batch thresholds and numerical-resolution criteria. Those thresholds still need to be fixed before the pilot and audited for sensitivity. The pilot must verify deterministic native routing, capacity acceptance, task-only gradients, preservation of model and RNG state, and precision before scaling. Any changed selections will remain feasibility evidence rather than evidence of improved pruning performance.
