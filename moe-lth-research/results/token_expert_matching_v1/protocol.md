# Controlled token-to-routing-slot matching v1

Hypothesis: disrupting learned token-to-routing-slot matching impairs sparse recovery more than dense recovery. This is not an established explanation or a novelty claim.

All five source seeds 7,17,29,43,61, original 32E dense and own IMP80/85 variants are retained, including winners and nonwinners. The audited factorial manifest, input inventory, mask provenance, original complete R0 states, native two-segment recovery and materialized data hashes are inherited without modification. The original persistent expert masks and unpruned biases remain. Each variant supplies its own donor schedule; dense donors are shared across the two sparsity comparisons. No source training or IMP discovery is repeated.

5000 attempted updates in two calls of 2500 preserve the historical fresh optimizer/scaler/RNG reset. Original optimizer, FP16, TF32, auxiliary weight .1, full 32-batch validation every 50 attempts, and materialized batch sequence remain native. Primary readout uses native inference on the full original validation stream with the frozen recipient router; training schedules are not applied to validation batches. Record this policy separately from training intervention and compare all replay conditions under this same readout.

Identity recipients use detached donor gates and externally fixed assignments and acceptance. Shuffled recipients apply a bijection of all token positions in each complete batch/layer, moving expert, gate and accepted status together. Proposed and accepted counts, per-expert gate multisets, drop totals and expert evaluations are exactly preserved. Which tokens are dropped and which token-specific coefficients they receive change. The estimand concerns token-to-routing-slot matching, not assignments alone.

Permutation draws are fixed before outcomes: 101,202,303. A SHA256 key of draw, source seed, global attempted step and layer initializes a local CPU Torch generator. Variant/sparsity never enter the key. Corresponding sparse/dense conditions have the same bijection independent of all training RNGs. Saved permutation checksums identify each draw.

Both replay conditions freeze router parameters before optimizer construction. Experts and all unique shared parameters are trainable. The task gate-coefficient path into router and upstream hidden states is removed. Expert computation and shared residual/attention/embedding task paths remain differentiable. Native recipient router probabilities and native proposed usage compute the auxiliary loss, including gradients through the frozen router into its inputs and earlier components. Frozen router parameters do not imply fixed native decisions; replay dispatch is externally fixed independently of those decisions. No surrogate/straight-through gate gradient is used. Ordinary native recovery is contextual, not the causal control.

Passive donor capture must reproduce its historical complete CE trajectory and final model exactly; it stores step/batch/token identity, expert IDs, gate dtype/values, acceptance, capacity, token positions and native accepted evaluation order, checksums and donor provenance. Diagnostic snapshots cannot fill missing schedules. Identity forward equality applies only at identical donor state/batch/RNG, never to the full replay training trajectory.

Pilot is seed17 dense/IMP80, identity and draw101, preceded by own donor capture. Expansion requires all mandatory numerical validations and an explicit feasibility report confirming finite stable learning and informative identity replay relative to native recovery. Pilot acceptance never depends on the sign of the shuffled sparse penalty. Severe learning-regime change stops expansion. No automatic follow-up intervention is authorized.

For each sparsity/seed: sparse_penalty=mean(three sparse shuffled CE)-sparse identity CE; dense_penalty=mean(three dense shuffled CE)-dense identity CE; differential=sparse_penalty-dense_penalty. Primary endpoint 5000 attempted updates; secondary 2500 and all native evaluation curves. Average draws within source seed, then report equally weighted seed means, descriptive SD/range/95% t intervals and all individual seeds. The two sparsities and common dense controls are dependent; n=5, never n=15 or token/expert/layer counts. Do not fill missing draws or promote pilot estimates to cohort conclusions.

Positive differential supports greater sparse sensitivity under this replay protocol only. It cannot establish assignment identity alone, IMP mask information, the historical cause of sparse wins, or generality beyond this cohort. Do not attribute a percentage of freezing penalties to this changed backward regime. If consistent, recommend but do not launch a follow-up preserving token gates/drop status while changing accepted expert identities with constrained exposure counts.

Execution uses existing Viper/Adder transport, canonical Torch2.7.0, per-host exclusive locks and at least24GiB free GPU admission. Preserve other jobs and every historical artifact. Fresh attempts only; errors preserve traces/checkpoints/status and require explicit review rather than automatic retry. Native resumable segment state is retained. Expansion is gated until pilot feasibility and measured compute/storage estimates exist.
