Gate-weighted exposure and historical composition are unavailable. Upper/lower exposure quartiles are within layer. Layer0 has a stronger contrast than later layers; all layer contrasts and exact IDs are retained. Choices in design.json were frozen after inventory and before matching/outcomes.

Reuse the verified training-only native FP32 task-gradient arrays and their split-half reliability/traffic. A is mean batchwise absolute weight-times-gradient, distinct from absolute signed mean or per-token sensitivity. No exposure-normalized importance is used. No-traffic sensitivity would be unmeasured. Validation never enters selection. Currently-small is bottom half in each fc1/fc2 tensor; low A is bottom quarter within50 magnitude strata. Pair eight low/high experts by exposure rank. A bounded nearest-neighbor pass matches magnitudes and raw A with equal dose and layer/projection allocation. All five sources must pass fixed gates or stop before losses; no exact row/column constraint, threshold sweep or expensive optimizer. Structural/history/function residuals remain confounds. Full definitions/tolerances are in immutable design.json.

Prior growth predicted small immediate sensitivity but mixed final damage. Reliable gradients have not yet established pruning utility. Exact-column matching was infeasible; this design does not recreate it. Router adaptation benefits sparse recovery without proving an exposure mechanism. The intervention tests a compound group-selection rule; historical exposure is observational and expert function/structure/history may confound it. Even consistent final L-H>0 does not prove waiting makes weights useful or saves compute. Mixed effects do not justify exposure-aware protection here. No pruning algorithm, classifier, winner selection or schedule is implemented.
