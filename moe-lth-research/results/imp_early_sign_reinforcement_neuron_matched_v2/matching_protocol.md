# E32 neuron-matched sign-flip design

The intervention changes starting signs on IMP survivors. The high arm uses the top quartile of reinforcement as ranked by v1 within each dense eligible matrix, then intersected with the IMP mask. The probe score is the initial weight sign times the sum of adaptive AdamW updates divided by the sum of their absolute values over 100 applied updates. Adaptive updates exclude decoupled weight decay. Eligible weights have finite nonzero initial values, finite scores, and finite positive update activity.

The control proposals use the same eligible IMP-survivor population. Each accepted rectangle contributes opposite diagonal edges to the high arm and the remaining edges to the random arm. Proposals alternate row and column orientations and draw eligible neighbors at random, including candidates from the top quartile. The two high edges must qualify for the high arm. Control proposals use starting sign, magnitude octile, update-activity octile, and energy constraints, with no ranking by reinforcement score. The per-matrix multiset of those covariate strata is identical. Each accepted rectangle preserves exact counts for each individual row and column.

The dose allocation selects prefixes of complete rectangles using only perturbation energy and the v1 relative matrix dose weights. The search evaluates the declared dose grid in descending order across all 18 pairs and retains the highest common passing dose. In the pair summary, coverage is the fraction of matrices with flips and neuron coverage is the mean within-matrix fraction of affected neurons. Energy differences are measured in squared L2 units. Relative neuron energy gaps divide the absolute gap by the larger arm energy, with zero assigned when both arms have zero energy. RMS gaps include hit neurons. The detail archives contain per-neuron counts and arm energies, worst relative and absolute gaps, overlap, and reinforcement quantiles. Matrix identities are the parameter names used by the IMP mask.

The initial exchange-based search at dose 0.34207 preserved exact counts and covariate strata but moved too few connections to pass the score-separation gate. Its complete target-dose audit is retained. A conditioned rectangle sampler was then tested using the saved probe and mask artifacts before starting any v2 recovery. The proposal implementation caps per-neuron relative energy gaps at 0.12 at each accepted prefix and per-matrix squared-energy imbalance at 0.015. It also caps within-matrix mean magnitude and activity imbalances at 0.04 and 0.09, respectively. The feasibility search uses one independent seeded draw sequence per seed, order, draw, and matrix. The manifests give the acceptance thresholds, proposal budget, namespace, and artifact hashes. The detail files give the numeric per-matrix sampler seeds.
